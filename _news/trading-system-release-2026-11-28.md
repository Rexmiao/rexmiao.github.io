Released the open-source [Lévy Quant Trading System]({{ '/projects/levyquant-trading/' | relative_url }}), a local back end for U.S. stocks that runs on your own computer against a moomoo paper account, with alert-only, confirm-before-send, and automatic modes. Paper trading only. Live trading is not implemented. Early stage: most behavior is verified by offline tests, not yet on the real service. [Source code and setup](https://github.com/Rexmiao/levyquant-trading).
