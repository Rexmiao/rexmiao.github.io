怎样清洗并构造方差

为什么在拟合前清洗

方差取决于收益的平方，错误报价会放大误差，重复记录和缺失值也会改变取样窗口。在外汇数据里，用前一笔报价填补 ND 会人为加入一次零变化。在美股日线里，跨过缺失的收盘价会把多日变化当成单日收益。分钟线按完整的 5 分钟区间计算盘中方差，再用隔夜端点和跨来源检查判断能否构造全日方差。这些规则统一了拟合和评估所用观测的口径。若行情缺失集中在高波动时段，保留的样本仍可能偏向数据较完整的交易日。

外汇参考汇率

跳过原表的 ND，校验日期唯一且按序、报价有限且为正。节假日和缺报价日期保持空缺，连续两次有效报价间计算对数变化，相同报价保留为零变化。用 2015 年 12 月的五次预热报价，计算最近五次对数变化的平方均值，再乘 252 得到年化方差代理量。每一步对应一个公布的报价，相邻方差窗口共享四个对数变化。

美股日线

核对标的、美元计价、纽约时区及日线频率。保持日期唯一有序，校验正价格、OHLC 高低关系和非负整数成交量。空行情行跳过，拆股和分红事件保存在单独文件。用相邻交易所交易日的复权收盘价计算对数收益，并将其平方作为日度方差代理量。任何一个收盘端点缺失，该笔收益保持缺失。

SNDK 日线实例。 2026-11-25 的日线报价有效，保留在数据中。前一个交易日 11 月 24 日的报价不完整，因此 11 月 25 日的复权收盘到收盘收益留空。

分钟线推算的全日方差

  1. 核对标的、UTC 时间戳和分钟网格，拒绝重复时间戳。按 XNYS 交易日历转为纽约时间，只取常规交易时段，并按日历处理提前收市。
  2. 用开盘整分钟 K 线的开盘价作为起点。每个 5 分钟区间取最后一根开盘与收盘价格有限且为正、成交量为正的分钟 K 线的收盘价。每个区间都必须有价，开盘整分钟也必须有效。缺失区间保持空缺，不做前向填充或插值。
  3. 将相邻取样价的对数收益平方求和得到盘中已实现方差。用 Yahoo 日线数据构造隔夜代理，先把当日开盘价乘以当日复权收盘与收盘价之比，再计算相对上一交易日复权收盘价的对数收益平方。两项相加得到全日代理。
  4. 如果隔夜所需日线端点缺失，或分钟线开盘至收盘对数收益与 Yahoo 日线同一时段收益相差超过 50 个基点，全日方差留空。能算出的盘中方差和剔除原因仍会保留。

AMD 清洗实例。 2,718 个交易日里，8 日因缺少 5 分钟区间而无法计算盘中方差。其余 2,710 日中，28 日触发 50 基点规则，1 个预热日缺少上一交易日的复权收盘价，最终得到 2,681 个全日方差观测。

How the data was cleaned and turned into variance

Why clean the data before fitting?

Variance uses squared returns, so a bad quote can magnify errors. Duplicate or missing records can also change the sampling window. Carrying an FX quote forward over an ND would add an artificial zero change. Skipping a missing equity close would treat a multi-session change as one day's return. Complete five-minute bins support the intraday estimate, and overnight endpoints and cross-source checks determine whether full-day variance is available. These rules keep the inputs consistent between fitting and evaluation. If data gaps cluster in volatile periods, the retained sample can favor better-covered sessions.

FX reference rates

The parser skips ND values, requires unique ordered dates, and validates finite positive rates. Gaps stay open. Log changes use consecutive valid fixes, and unchanged fixes retain a zero change. Five December 2015 warm-up fixes seed the calculation. Annualized proxy variance is 252 times the mean of the five latest squared log changes. A step is one published fix, so adjacent variance windows share four changes.

Daily equity prices

The downloader checks each ticker, USD denomination, New York timezone, and daily frequency. It checks ordered unique dates, positive prices, OHLC ranges, and integer volume of zero or greater. Rows with null quotes are skipped. Split and dividend events go to a separate file. Squared log changes between adjusted closes of adjacent exchange sessions form the daily variance proxy. A missing close leaves the associated returns missing.

SNDK daily example. The valid 2026-11-25 quote stays in the data. The prior session's quote on November 24 is incomplete, so the November 25 adjusted close-to-close return stays missing.

Full-day variance from minute data

  1. Check the symbol, UTC timestamps, and minute alignment, and reject duplicate bar starts. Use regular New York sessions on the XNYS calendar, including early closes.
  2. Start at the exact opening bar's open. For each five-minute bin, use the close from the last minute bar with finite positive open and close prices and positive volume. A valid opening bar and full bin coverage are required. Leave missing bins empty.
  3. Sum squared adjacent log returns to get regular-session realized variance. For the overnight proxy, multiply the current Yahoo open by its adjusted-close-to-close ratio, then square its log return from the prior session's adjusted close. Add the two components for the full-day variance proxy.
  4. Leave full-day variance missing if a required overnight endpoint is absent or if the minute and Yahoo open-to-close log returns differ by more than 50 basis points. Retain intraday variance when available and record the exclusion reason.

AMD cleaning example. Of 2,718 exchange sessions, 8 had missing five-minute bins. Of the other 2,710, 28 exceeded the 50-basis-point threshold and the first session lacked a prior adjusted close for warm-up. That left 2,681 full-day variance values.

哪些日期进入美股评估

在共同交易日历跨度的前 80% 拟合参数。评分要求每个预测起点及之前共 22 个交易日、以及之后 22 个交易日的数据在同组标的上连续可用。评估至少需要 252 个训练观测及 84 个样本外预测起点。各模型在同一标的组和数据口径内使用相同的预测起点。预测目标为未来 1、5 或 22 个交易日的平均日度方差乘以 252。

  • 七个长期标的。 SPY、NVDA、JPM、AMD、QQQ、AVGO、MU 的日线有 527 个共同预测起点。分钟线的全日方差在 2,624 日共同可用，经过连续窗口及样本外筛选后保留 190 个预测起点。最晚起点是 2025-12-30，原始分钟档案截至 2026-10-23。
  • NBIS。 从 2024-10-21 复牌起纳入，单独评估。日线有 85 个预测起点，分钟线样本不足。
  • SNDK。 从 2025-02-24 分拆上市起纳入，两种频率的样本跨度都不足。日线仍保留 2026-11-20 的拆股事件记录，11-20、11-23 和 11-24 的行情缺失按交易日历保留。

Which dates enter the equity evaluation

Fit parameters on the first 80% of the common calendar span. An eligible origin requires 22 consecutive sessions through that date and 22 subsequent sessions, all usable across the tickers in its group. An evaluation needs at least 252 training observations and 84 out-of-sample origins. Models share the same origins within each ticker group and frequency. The target is 252 times the mean daily variance over the next 1, 5, or 22 trading sessions.

  • Seven long-history tickers. SPY, NVDA, JPM, AMD, QQQ, AVGO, and MU share 527 daily-data origins. The minute-derived full-day measure is available across all seven on 2,624 sessions. The contiguous-window and holdout rules leave 190 common origins, ending 2025-12-30. The raw archive ends 2026-10-23.
  • NBIS. Its separate evaluation starts from the 2024-10-21 trading resumption. The daily sample yields 85 forecast origins. The minute-derived sample is too short.
  • SNDK. Its sample starts with the 2025-02-24 spin-off listing. Both frequencies have insufficient span. The daily source retains the 2026-11-20 split event and leaves gaps in quotes on November 20, 23, and 24.

怎样核查和复现

公开的外汇 CSV 和来源 JSON 保留建模观测、预热报价、快照日期和观测文件的 SHA-256 校验值。可以用公开脚本从保存的 H.10 原始表重建 CSV，也可以用现有 CSV 直接重算模型。美联储可能修订历史报价，重新下载后可对比校验值。

美股行情快照和处理日志保存在本地研究目录。清单记录来源与校验值，逐日审计保留有效区间数、覆盖率、跨源偏差和剔除原因。网页公开汇总评分和从原始评估结果生成网页 JSON 的脚本。原始美股行情遵循各来源的再分发条款。

How to verify and reproduce the results

The public FX CSV and provenance JSON provide the model observations, warm-up fixes, snapshot dates, and a SHA-256 checksum. Rebuild the CSV with the public script and a saved H.10 source table or rerun the model from the included CSV. The Board can revise past rates, so compare checksums after a new download.

Equity snapshots and processing logs live in the local research project. Manifests record sources and checksums. Daily audits retain effective bin counts, coverage, cross-source discrepancies, and exclusion reasons. The site publishes aggregate scores and the script that exports them from the benchmark outputs. Raw equity quotes are subject to each source's redistribution terms.
