Project Progress: A Trading System, New Domains, and a Daily Briefing

November 28, 2026

2026

Today I released the open-source LévyQuant Trading System, moved my projects onto a shared levyquant.ai domain, added the LévyQuant Daily Briefing to the portfolio, and gave each project app a consistent day/night display.

LévyQuant Trading System

  • Released the LévyQuant Trading System as an open-source repository under the MIT license. It is a local trading back end for U.S. stocks that runs on your own computer against a moomoo paper account. It captures 5-minute bars, produces signals, applies limits you set yourself, sends orders, and reconciles every order with the broker, in alert-only, confirm-before-send, or fully automatic mode.
  • Status: Paper trading only. Live trading is not implemented. Early stage: most behavior is verified by offline tests, not yet on the real service.

Personal Site

  • The site now lives at rexy.levyquant.ai. Old rexmiao.github.io links, including project pages, redirect automatically.
  • The homepage now shows the Trading System beside Alpha Lab.
  • Added the LévyQuant Daily Briefing to Projects as a pilot, with a direct link to the briefing site and an animated diagram of its pipeline from selected public sources through LLM editing and translation to email.
  • Demo links now use the new project domains: Lévy Volatility Lab and Alpha Lab.
  • Published two bilingual Trading Reflections: a framework for using GEX and Max Pain across indices and expirations, and a review of QQQ and SPY 0DTE trades on November 27.
  • Long formulas in reflections no longer widen the page on phones.

LévyQuant Daily Briefing

  • The briefing site moved to daily.levyquant.ai, and the brand is now written LévyQuant.
  • Added a day/night switch, with day mode as the default.
  • Delivery is more efficient and more careful near the sending limit: batched delivery records, one mail login per run, and an admin warning as the recipient list approaches its cap.
  • Confirmation and unsubscribe emails are moving to the new domain.

Alpha Lab

  • The research interface is now at alphalab.levyquant.ai.
  • Releases 0.12.5 to 0.12.7 added a day/night toggle in the header, with day mode as the default.

PCR Market Watch

  • Releases v0.2.19 to v0.2.21 added a dark theme, a PCR snapshot strip, a refreshed summary table, and a day/night display mode, and prepared the app for a custom domain.

Lévy Volatility Lab

  • The Hugging Face demo is now served at volatility.levyquant.ai and was republished from the site’s source.
