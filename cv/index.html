Research Profile

  • Applied mathematics and computational science researcher working at the intersection of medical imaging, computational neuroscience, statistical modeling, and biomedical artificial intelligence. Current research develops multimodal and longitudinal methods for understanding brain disease progression and extends these approaches toward scalable multimodal AI, large language models, and foundation models for precision medicine.
  • Research experience spans longitudinal statistical modeling, multimodal data integration, numerical methods, and scientific software development. Master's research addressed volatility modeling using Lévy processes and Ornstein–Uhlenbeck models in the context of option pricing. Interested in applying rigorous empirical research and computational modeling to quantitative investment research.
